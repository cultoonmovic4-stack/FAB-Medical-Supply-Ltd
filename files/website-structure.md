# FAB Medical Supplies Ltd. --- Website Structure

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed sitemap and page content organization
for the frontend-only FAB Medical Supplies Ltd. website. It is a
planning proposal; page details and public claims should be checked
against information approved by the owner.

FAB is a Uganda-based medical equipment procurement, importation, and
supply business. FAB does not manufacture the equipment.

## 2. Proposed Sitemap

``` text
Home
├── About Us
├── Products
│   ├── All Products / Catalogue
│   ├── Product Category
│   └── Individual Product Detail
├── Services
├── Who We Serve
└── Contact
```

Proposed main navigation: Home, About Us, Products, Services, Who We
Serve, Contact. A prominent Contact FAB or Enquire action may lead to
the Contact page or an owner-approved direct contact channel. It is not
an online checkout.

## 3. Page-by-Page Structure

### 3.1 Home

**Purpose:** Introduce FAB, establish credibility, and guide visitors to
the catalogue or direct contact.

Proposed sections: 1. **Hero / introduction:** A clear headline about
medical equipment procurement, importation, and supply; brief supporting
copy; Explore Products and Contact FAB actions; genuine approved
photography. 2. **Company introduction:** Short, owner-approved overview
with a link to About Us. 3. **Browse equipment categories:** Curated
selection of approved categories, each linking to its catalogue view and
using real images where available. 4. **Featured products:** A small
selection of approved individual products, each linking to its detail
view. 5. **Procurement and supply overview:** Explain confirmed
activities without describing FAB as a manufacturer. 6. **Who we serve
preview:** Briefly introduce the identified customer groups and link to
Who We Serve. 7. **Contact call-to-action:** Invite visitors to contact
FAB directly using approved contact channels. 8. **Footer:** Company
name, navigation, approved contact details, and copyright.

Section order can be refined during design as long as key visitor tasks
remain easy to find.

### 3.2 About Us

**Purpose:** Explain who FAB is and what the company does using factual,
owner-approved information.

Proposed sections: 1. Page introduction 2. Company overview 3.
Procurement, importation, and supply focus 4. Company approach or
values, only if supplied and approved 5. Optional history, only if
verified 6. Direct-contact call-to-action

Do not invent founding dates, years of experience, mission statements,
accreditations, or market-leadership claims.

### 3.3 Products

**Purpose:** Let visitors browse the catalogue and view individual
products.

**All Products / Catalogue view** - Page title and concise
introduction - Category navigation or filtering based on the approved
catalogue - Product grid using actual product photographs - Approved
product names and optional verified short summaries - Link to each
product detail view - Direct enquiry/contact action

Add search, sorting, or filtering only if the final catalogue size makes
them useful. Do not add nonfunctional controls.

**Product category view** - Category title and short approved
introduction - Products in that category - Consistent product cards -
Navigation back to the catalogue or other categories

Use category names from the owner's approved catalogue and supplied
company material; do not invent categories to fill a layout.

**Individual product detail view** - Product name - Large actual product
image; additional images only when supplied - Verified description -
Technical specifications only when supplied and approved - Category
label - Direct enquiry/contact action, such as "Ask about this
product" - Return link to category or catalogue

Do not display prices, stock status, delivery estimates, warranty
details, or manufacturer names unless confirmed by the owner.

### 3.4 Services

**Purpose:** Explain the services FAB actually offers.

Confirmed business activities for the website are procurement,
importation, and supply. Installation, maintenance, and servicing were
mentioned as possible additional offerings but remain unconfirmed.

Proposed sections: 1. Page introduction 2. Confirmed procurement,
importation, and supply information 3. Additional service blocks only
after owner confirmation 4. Contact call-to-action

If the owner confirms installation, maintenance, or servicing, describe
the exact scope and any limitations he provides. Otherwise, omit those
claims.

### 3.5 Who We Serve

**Purpose:** Help visitors identify whether FAB serves their type of
customer or facility.

Audience groups identified during planning: - Patients and individual
customers - Hospitals - Clinics - Government health facilities - NGOs -
Laboratories - Theatres - Pharmacies

Proposed sections: 1. Page introduction 2. Audience/facility group cards
or an organized list 3. Brief explanatory copy based only on confirmed
supply capabilities 4. Links to relevant catalogue categories where a
genuine connection is known 5. Direct-contact call-to-action

Do not imply clinical advice or that every product is suitable for every
audience.

### 3.6 Contact

**Purpose:** Give visitors clear, direct ways to reach FAB.

**This page must not contain a contact form.**

Proposed sections: 1. Page title and short invitation to get in touch 2.
Direct contact options using owner-approved details:
telephone/click-to-call, WhatsApp if approved, and email/mailto link 3.
Business address and map only if verified and approved 4. Optional
business hours only if supplied 5. Optional note inviting visitors to
mention the equipment they are asking about

Do not invent phone numbers, email addresses, hours, or a precise
location.

## 4. Shared Site Elements

### Header

-   Approved FAB logo asset
-   Main navigation
-   Current-page indication where appropriate
-   Keyboard-accessible mobile navigation
-   Easy access to Products and Contact

### Footer

-   Company name and approved short description
-   Main page links
-   Approved contact information
-   Address and social links only if supplied and verified
-   Copyright line

### Product card

-   Actual product image
-   Product name
-   Optional concise approved description
-   Link to product detail view

Do not use unsupported badges such as "Best Seller," "In Stock,"
"Certified," or "New" unless the owner supplies accurate data.

## 5. Intended User Journeys

-   **Looking for an item:** Home → Products → Category → Product detail
    → Direct contact
-   **Learning about FAB:** Home → About Us / Services → Products →
    Contact
-   **Checking customer fit:** Home → Who We Serve → Products → Contact

These are suggested paths, not restrictions. Visitors should be able to
reach the catalogue and contact details from relevant pages.

## 6. Content and Assets Needed

Collect before finalizing page content: - Official logo file in suitable
quality and format - Approved company introduction and any verified
history - Final product/category inventory - Actual high-quality product
images matched to product names - Approved product descriptions and
technical specifications - Confirmed list and scope of services -
Current phone, WhatsApp, email, and address details - Approved business
hours, social links, and other public contact channels

## 7. Decisions to Revisit

-   Confirm final navigation labels with the owner.
-   Confirm whether Services should be a separate page if confirmed
    service information is brief.
-   Confirm whether Who We Serve needs a standalone page or can be
    covered adequately on Home.
-   Choose category routes versus filtered catalogue views after
    reviewing the product inventory.
-   Choose dedicated product detail routes versus a reusable detail view
    based on catalogue size and usability.
