# FAB Medical Supplies Ltd. --- Website Project

## 1. Project Overview

This project is a frontend-only website for FAB Medical Supplies Ltd., a
Uganda-based business involved in the procurement, importation, and
supply of medical equipment.

The website will present the company professionally, help visitors
explore its equipment catalogue, and make it easy for prospective
customers to contact the business directly.

## 2. Business Summary

FAB Medical Supplies Ltd. supplies medical equipment to a broad range of
customers and healthcare organizations, including:

-   Patients and individual customers
-   Hospitals and clinics
-   Government health facilities
-   NGOs
-   Laboratories
-   Theatres
-   Pharmacies

The company information and equipment categories provided in the company
PDF will be used as source material for the website. Product names,
descriptions, specifications, and service claims should be checked
against information supplied or approved by the owner before
publication.

**Business clarification:** FAB does not manufacture the equipment. The
business procures/imports and supplies equipment.

Installation, maintenance, and servicing were mentioned as possible
offerings, but should be included as website services only after the
owner confirms the exact scope.

## 3. Website Goals

The website is intended to:

1.  Establish a credible and professional online presence for FAB
    Medical Supplies Ltd.
2.  Explain the company's procurement, importation, and supply business.
3.  Let visitors browse equipment by category and view individual
    products with actual product images.
4.  Help relevant customers identify equipment that may meet their
    needs.
5.  Make direct contact with FAB easy through the contact details and
    communication links approved by the owner.

## 4. Project Scope

### Included

-   Responsive frontend website
-   Company information and presentation
-   Product categories and individual product display pages or views
-   Actual high-quality product photographs supplied by the company
-   Contact page with direct contact options
-   Navigation and user-friendly browsing experience
-   Frontend implementation using React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS

### Not included in this version

-   Backend or server-side application
-   Database
-   Customer accounts or authentication
-   Online checkout or payment processing
-   Contact form on the Contact page
-   Admin dashboard or content management system

The contact page will provide direct contact methods rather than an
on-page form. Any quotation action should use an owner-approved direct
channel, such as a telephone, email, or WhatsApp link, once those
details are confirmed.

## 5. Brand and Design Direction

The website will follow FAB's existing identity as shown in the company
logo supplied in the PDF. The core colors are the logo's blue, red, and
white. Exact digital color values should be sampled from the approved
logo asset before being recorded in the design guidelines.

The design should be human-made in appearance: clear, purposeful,
professional, and appropriate for a medical equipment supplier. Avoid
AI-generated visual assets and generic AI-style effects. Detailed visual
rules will be maintained in `design-guidelines.md`.

## 6. Technology

-   **Frontend framework:** React
-   **Build tool:** Vite
-   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
-   **Backend:** None for this version

The product catalogue should be organized in a maintainable frontend
data structure so that approved product information and images can be
added or updated without redesigning the whole site.

## 8. Content and Decisions to Confirm

Before publishing, confirm with the owner:

-   Official company description and preferred wording
-   Current contact details and business address
-   Exact service offerings, including whether installation,
    maintenance, and servicing are provided
-   Approved product list, names, descriptions, and technical
    specifications
-   Which product images correspond to each product
-   Any manufacturer or supplier brands that may be named or displayed
-   Delivery coverage and any other operational claims the company wants
    to make

Do not invent product specifications, certifications, partnerships,
service guarantees, customer testimonials, or company history.
